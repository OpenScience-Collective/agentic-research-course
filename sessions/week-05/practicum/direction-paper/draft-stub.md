## Working title

Neural Correlates of Naturalistic Movie Watching: An Electroencephalographic Mini-Review Across Psychophysics, Action, Language, and Emotion.

## Working thesis

EEG under naturalistic movie viewing tracks all four perspectives, but with sharply different signal-to-noise across them. The psychophysics layer is well established and almost closed; language and action are emerging with clear translation paths from the dominant fMRI literature; emotion remains the noisiest layer, with EEG-translation work sparse and developmental cohorts absent. The Healthy Brain Network (HBN) developmental dataset is the natural test bed for the next layer of work.

## Notes for the agent

- **No claim without a card.** If you cannot link to a `collection/<strand>/<slug>/card.md`, do not state the claim. Move it to a "questions for the next collection pass" list and surface it in `synthesis/gap-analysis-stub.md`.
- **Thematic, not chronological.** Group by perspective and by sub-theme within perspective; do not order paragraphs by year unless the timeline is itself the argument.
- **Citation weaving.** Mix integral ("Hasson and colleagues showed...") and non-integral ("...has been shown in naturalistic viewing [card]"). Use synthesis ("multiple lines of evidence converge on...") and contrast ("X reports A, while Y reports the opposite") where the corpus supports them.
- **Bias-aware.** A finding that contradicts the working thesis is more valuable than one that confirms it. Surface contradicting findings explicitly; do not paraphrase them out of the prose.
