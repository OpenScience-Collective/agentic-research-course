# Strand B, Action (Phase 1 brief)

**Goal.** Populate `collection/action/` with at least 5 paper-cards covering action observation under naturalistic movie viewing. The strand answers: when participants watch movies containing depicted actions (reaching, grasping, locomotion, social actions), what motor- and parietal-cortex responses are recoverable from EEG, and how do they scale with action category, agency, and temporal grain?

## Scope

Cover four sub-areas.

### 1. Action observation in naturalistic viewing

- Hasson et al. 2008, neurocinematics; the framing paper.
- Bartels & Zeki 2004, natural viewing functional magnetic resonance imaging (fMRI) with action segmentation.
- More recent EEG / magnetoencephalography (MEG) work on action-locked responses under continuous viewing.

### 2. Mu-rhythm suppression and motor mirroring

- Mu-rhythm (8-13 Hz central) suppression during action observation; classic Pineda reviews.
- Naturalistic-viewing extensions of mu suppression; reliability across long viewing sessions.

### 3. Event segmentation and boundary detection

- Zacks event-segmentation theory papers; behavioural and neural correlates.
- EEG signatures of event boundaries during continuous viewing.
- Connection to scene-cut responses (cross-reference Strand A).

### 4. Social actions and agency

- Social vs non-social action contrasts in naturalistic stimuli.
- Agent-detection and biological-motion processing during movies.

## Per-entry deliverable

Create folder `collection/action/<slug>/` containing:

- Animal-model work
