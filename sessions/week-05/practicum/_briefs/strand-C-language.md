# Strand C, Language (Phase 1 brief)

**Goal.** Populate `collection/language/` with at least 5 paper-cards covering speech and narrative comprehension under naturalistic movie viewing. The strand answers: when participants watch movies with dialogue, voiceover, or narration, what speech-elicited and narrative-locked responses are recoverable from EEG, and how do they map onto the semantic / syntactic / pragmatic hierarchy?

## Scope

Cover four sub-areas.

### 1. Speech-elicited responses in continuous listening

- mTRF Toolbox papers (Crosse et al. 2016, 2021); reliability of speech tracking.
- Phonemic and envelope tracking under naturalistic listening; ages and clinical populations.
- Speech-in-movie variants where speech is concurrent with visual narrative.

### 2. Narrative comprehension and the hierarchy of timescales

- Lerner et al. 2011, hierarchy of timescales in language; cite even if fMRI.
- Brennan et al. 2016, EEG narrative comprehension.
- Subsequent EEG work on phrase- and sentence-level integration during continuous narrative.

### 3. Semantic networks during continuous viewing

- Huth et al. 2016, semantic maps; cite as the target framework.
- EEG / MEG analogues; word-level and concept-level tracking on naturalistic transcripts.

### 4. Bilingual and developmental considerations

- Naturalistic-viewing studies in non-native speakers.
- Age-of-acquisition and developmental trajectories of speech tracking; relevant for HBN cohorts.

## Per-entry deliverable

Create folder `collection/language/<slug>/` containing:

- `card.md` from the schema (`type: paper`, `strand: language`)
- `source.pdf` only when redistributable
- `source.md` always required
- `meta.json` with provenance
- BibTeX entry appended to `collection/language/language.bib`
- One-line entry appended to `collection/language/INDEX.md`

## Skills to use

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] >=5 entries across the four sub-areas
- [ ] Every entry folder has `card.md`, `source.md`, and `meta.json`
- [ ] `source.pdf` archived for >=60% of redistributable entries
- [ ] BibTeX in `language.bib` and INDEX populated

## Out of scope

- Static reading paradigms (single sentence on a screen)
- Speech-production work
- Pure psycholinguistic priming paradigms unrelated to continuous viewing
