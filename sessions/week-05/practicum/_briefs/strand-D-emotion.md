# Strand D, Emotion (Phase 1 brief)

**Goal.** Populate `collection/emotion/` with at least 5 paper-cards covering affective dynamics under naturalistic movie viewing. The strand answers: when participants watch emotionally evocative movies, what valence- and arousal-tracking signals are recoverable from EEG, how synchronised are they across viewers, and what is the time course relative to scene-level affective annotations?

## Scope

Cover four sub-areas.

### 1. Affect time-course tracking

- Continuous valence and arousal ratings; behavioural-EEG concordance.
- Frontal alpha asymmetry and valence; reliability under naturalistic viewing.
- Skin conductance and pupillometry as complementary channels; multi-modal tracking.

### 2. Discrete emotion classification under naturalistic stimuli

- Saarimaki et al. 2016, discrete emotions in naturalistic stimuli; foundational fMRI work.
- Kragel et al. 2019, fMRI emotion classifiers across naturalistic stimuli.
- EEG / MEG analogues; cross-decoding studies.

### 3. Inter-subject affective synchronisation

- Nummenmaa et al. 2012, emotion synchronisation across viewers.
- EEG ISC restricted to emotionally salient segments; Dmochowski follow-ups.

### 4. Developmental and clinical considerations

- Affective response to naturalistic stimuli across childhood and adolescence (HBN-relevant).
- Atypical-development variants (autism spectrum, attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder) and their effect on naturalistic-stimulus affective tracking.

## Per-entry deliverable

Create folder `collection/emotion/<slug>/` containing:

- `card.md` from the schema (`type: paper`, `strand: emotion`)
- `source.pdf` only when redistributable
- `source.md` always required
- `meta.json` with provenance
- BibTeX entry appended to `collection/emotion/emotion.bib`
- One-line entry appended to `collection/emotion/INDEX.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] >=5 entries across the four sub-areas
- [ ] Every entry folder has `card.md`, `source.md`, and `meta.json`

## Out of scope

- Static-image affective stimuli (International Affective Picture System and similar)
- Mood-induction paradigms unrelated to naturalistic viewing
- Pure self-report measures with no neural data
