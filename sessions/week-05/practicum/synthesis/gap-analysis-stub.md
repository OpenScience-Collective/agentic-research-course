## Cross-strand patterns

- **Developmental coverage.** All four strands have the same gap: their canonical work is adult-only, and HBN's developmental cohort is uncovered by the EEG-translation papers. This is the most likely natural finding to surface during the live `/manuscript:paper-review` boomerang.
- **EEG translation lag.** Each strand has a clear fMRI anchor (Hasson 2004, Hasson 2008, Huth 2016, Saarimaki 2016) and a sparser EEG follow-up. The lag varies in length; psychophysics is the closest to closed, emotion the furthest.
